# Natural-sequence hidden distillation and memory regression

Three local teacher sequences were generated from project-authored training prompts under the normal DeepSeek chat template, using temperature 0.6 and top-p 0.95. None finished within 512 generated tokens. Two unfinished sequences trained the candidates; the third was reserved for local hidden-state comparison. Exact prompt overlap with the existing development scenarios was checked, but conceptual overlap remains possible. Token sequences remain local and are not evidence of correct or complete answers. Their metadata and hashes are in `runs/deepseek-natural-distill-data-20261111/summary.json`.

Starting from the existing layer-6/12 candidates, each replacement was extended to 768 positions and independently trained for 120 steps to match frozen original-layer outputs on the two natural sequences and 18 controlled memory questions. Six held memory examples and one 531-token natural sequence were used for local validation. On the held natural sequence, layer 6's relative residual MSE fell from 1.349 to 1.164, and layer 12's from 1.506 to 1.325. These are teacher-input hidden-state metrics, not answer scores. Training took 186.0 seconds and peaked at 3,787,477,504 allocated CUDA bytes. Candidate tensors remain local; complete settings, losses and hashes are in `runs/deepseek-spora-natural-hidden-distill-20261111/result.json`.

The actual memory comparison was unfavorable. On eight previously inspected questions with program-supplied addresses and time slots, the original paired candidate scored **6/8 exact**, while the newly distilled pair scored **1/8**. The integrated original layers scored 4/8 in the same run. The original pair thus remains the preferred checkpoint; the new pair should not be promoted. Per-question text is in `runs/deepseek-spora-natural-distill-memory-eval-20261111.json`.

Natural-reasoning generation on two previously inspected questions and one seed also did not demonstrate a gain: the first produced an unrelated answer after 215 tokens, while the second reached 512 tokens without a final answer. The original base had answered the first correctly with the same seed in the earlier sampling probe. These limited samples cannot establish an accuracy rate. Full final answers and settings are in `runs/deepseek-spora-natural-distill-generation-eval-20261111.json`; reasoning text is not recorded there.

The results demonstrate why hidden-state matching is an insufficient proxy for preserving the original individual's memory behavior. The teacher's decoder layers are not equivalent to the previously memory-answer-trained pair, and the local objective used teacher hidden inputs rather than the student's evolving distribution. A subsequent candidate should explicitly supervise memory answers and preserve prior behavior while measuring natural generation, then be rejected if either regresses. The candidate loader now validates and uses the checkpoint's declared position length; 49 targeted unit checks passed.
