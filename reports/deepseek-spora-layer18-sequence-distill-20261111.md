# Sequence distillation for the full layer-18 replacement

The previous experiment supervised only the first answer token. This run instead trained the full SPORA replacement using the teacher's distribution across every token of each short target, plus cross entropy. It began from the preceding curriculum candidate with layers 6 and 12 fixed. The teacher retained those two replacements and the original layer 18. Training used 36 memory questions and eight ordinary Chinese prompts; their targets contained 78 and 76 tokens respectively. Generic teacher continuations were limited to 10 tokens. The 120-step run took 423.2 seconds and peaked at 4,591,232,000 bytes of allocated CUDA memory.

On 12 previously used development questions, exact short-answer accuracy rose from 3/12 to 5/12. Only two answers changed from incorrect to correct; these entities and questions have been reused during development and do not establish generalization. Three general prompts were sampled before and after with a 12-token cap; two outputs remained identical and one changed. The cap truncates all three answers, so this is an inspection sample, not a general-language benchmark.

We also reran the existing four-entity/eight-question comparison, which has likewise been previously inspected during development. The paired system without layer 18 scored 6/8 exact, the 10% blend scored 6/8 and the full replacement scored 3/8. Those scores equal the prior candidate's results on this same comparison. This run therefore improves the older development set but does not demonstrate improvement on the second set or justify full replacement. Both comparisons supply memory addresses and time slots programmatically.

Full outputs and provenance are in `runs/deepseek-spora-layer18-sequence-distill-20261111/result.json` and `runs/deepseek-spora-sequence-fresh-entity-eval-20261111.json`. The candidate weights stay local. The first launch failed before optimization because teacher-generated labels were inference tensors; cloning them outside inference mode fixed the failure, and a minimal backward check succeeded before rerunning. Further work needs fresh, diverse held-out facts and full-length Chinese generation evaluation, together with memory-neutral and memory-enabled controls.
