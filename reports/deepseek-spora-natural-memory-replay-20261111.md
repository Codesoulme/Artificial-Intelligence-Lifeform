# Memory-answer replay after natural-sequence distillation

The natural hidden-distillation candidate regressed on the matched memory probe from 6/8 exact answers to 1/8. We tested whether direct answer supervision could recover behavior while constraining natural-prefix output distributions. The teacher for the natural-prefix KL was each run's starting SPORA pair, not the original DeepSeek layers. The four natural training prefixes came from two incomplete local teacher trajectories; only their last-token distributions were constrained.

The first replay ran 90 steps with 36 controlled memory questions and four natural prefixes of 128 or 192 tokens. It raised the previously inspected memory probe from 1/8 to 3/8 exact. On two previously inspected natural questions with one seed, it answered the post-office question correctly; the warm-water answer mentioned the right drink but added unsupported claims. One natural-generation attempt exited without a Python error or output file; a subsequent logged retry completed. The cause of the first exit was not established.

The second replay continued from that checkpoint for 120 steps, using 108 memory questions from three value-assignment sets and four natural prefixes. On the same eight-question development probe, the new pair scored 6/8 exact, matching the original pair's total but with a different error pattern: one earlier failure was corrected and one earlier success was lost. On six new names, six addresses absent from the replay training queries, new value assignments and 12 new question instances, the original pair scored 9/12 exact and the diverse-replay pair 8/12. The new pair corrected one original failure but introduced two new failures. These are still short questions in one beverage domain, with the address and temporal slot supplied by the evaluator; the 12-question probe becomes development data after this inspection.

The second candidate's natural generation restated the post-office fact and then repeated the question; the warm-water sample reached the 512-token limit without a final answer. This is one seed per question and is not an accuracy estimate. The results do not demonstrate preservation of general Chinese conversation or autonomous memory lookup, and do not justify replacing the original paired checkpoint. The original and both experimental checkpoint sets remain separate locally.

The first training run took 308.1 seconds with 4,884,207,616 peak allocated CUDA bytes; the second took 384.4 seconds with 4,885,651,456 peak allocated bytes. The targeted 49-test suite passed. Results and exact outputs are in `runs/deepseek-spora-replay-memory-eval-20261111.json`, `runs/deepseek-spora-replay-generation-eval-20261111.json`, `runs/deepseek-spora-diverse-memory-eval-20261111.json`, `runs/deepseek-spora-diverse-fresh-memory-eval-20261111.json` and `runs/deepseek-spora-diverse-generation-eval-20261111.json`; training provenance is in the corresponding local run directories. Full reasoning sequences and model weights are not committed.
