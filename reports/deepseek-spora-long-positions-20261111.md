# Extending SPORA replacement positions to 256

The tested layer-6/12/18 checkpoints had 128 learned absolute-position vectors. `SPORALayerReplacement.extend_positions` can now preserve those vectors exactly and append zero-initialized vectors; callers must extend before creating an optimizer because the position parameter is replaced. This changes the permitted range, not the demonstrated quality of the additional positions. Tail-only experimental checkpoints can be restored with `apply_position_tails`, which validates the base and candidate digests, dimensions and finite values before mutating any block.

In the zero-initialized probe, the three-layer model's short-input logits were bit-identical before and after extension. Two development prompts of 131 and 143 tokens ran through original DeepSeek, the extended pair and the extended triple; the memory reader was not active. On the shopping-list prompt the extended pair correctly named soy sauce, while the triple did not complete its answer within 24 tokens. On the meeting-place prompt all three answers were inaccurate or incomplete. Reference-answer NLL is recorded in `runs/deepseek-spora-long-position-probe-20261111.json`, but should not be mistaken for free-generation accuracy.

An exploratory 36-step run then trained only positions 128 onward of all three blocks on four project-authored long prompts, using the frozen base's first-token distribution and first-token answer supervision. Original positions 0–127 and all other weights remained frozen; their position hashes matched before and after. Only positions 128–170 received nonzero updates. Positions 171–255 remain untrained zeros. The two previously inspected development prompts had lower reference-answer NLL after training (11.67→11.34 and 14.06→13.33), but generated answers remained wrong: the shopping answer changed to tea and tissues rather than soy sauce, and the meeting-place answer confused dining with the meeting. This is a failed quality result, not evidence of successful long-context learning.

Training used 184.3 seconds and 4,449,793,024 peak allocated CUDA bytes. Results, prompts, hashes and the limitations are in `runs/deepseek-spora-long-position-train-20261111/result.json`; the local `position-tails.pt` holds only the three extra position tables and records source hashes. Restoring it against the three actual candidate checkpoints preserved every old vector and reproduced each stored tail. The targeted 48-test suite passed. Neither probe evaluates autonomous memory routing, multi-turn interaction, or general long-context competence.
